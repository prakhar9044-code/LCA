About this Tool

This is a simplified Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) calculator for electronic devices. It estimates greenhouse gas emissions (kg CO₂e) across four main stages.

1. Manufacturing

Energy required to produce the device multiplied by an emission factor for that electricity.

Manufacturing Impact = Manufacturing Energy (kWh) × Emission Factor (kg CO₂e/kWh)

2. Transportation

Emissions from moving the product from factory to user, based on weight and distance.

Transport Impact = Weight (tonnes) × Distance (km) × Emission Factor (kg CO₂e/tonne-km)

3. Use Phase

Electricity consumed while the device is used over its lifetime.

Yearly Energy = Power (kW) × Hours/day × 365
Use Impact = Yearly Energy × Lifetime (years) × Grid Emission Factor

4. End-of-Life

Higher recycling rate reduces remaining impact. Only the non-recycled fraction of base end-of-life emissions is counted.

EOL Impact = Base EOL Emissions × (1 − Recycling Rate / 100)

Total Carbon Footprint

Total = Manufacturing + Transportation + Use Phase + End-of-Life

Important notes

  • This tool is for educational and comparative purposes.
  • Emission factors are approximate defaults — replace with regional values when possible.
  • For official reporting, follow ISO 14040 / 14044.
  • Real LCAs also include materials extraction, packaging, and detailed disposal.
